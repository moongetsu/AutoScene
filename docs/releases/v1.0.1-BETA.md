exports a scenepack as one file with a chapter per shot.

## What's new

- **Characters without a visible face (optional).** Turn on **Settings →
  General → Find characters without a visible face** and AutoScene also
  follows people by their body and clothes. Shots where a character faces
  away, or is too small for their face, join their scenepack marked **No
  face**, after every shot with their face. **Only with their face** leaves
  them out, and a right-click on one says it isn't them (or isn't a person).
  The timeline shows these moments as faint spans. In a test episode the
  main cast gained about 6 % more shots.
- **A scenepack in one file.** The export now makes one video with a
  chapter per shot, its chapters named from a template. A switch in the
  export dialog brings back a file per shot. Every shot is still cut
  frame-exact and checked.
- **Right-click menus** on episodes, characters, shots and more, refreshed
  buttons, a progress pill on episode cards and a centred activity ring.
  Thanks to @moongetsu (#6–#9).

## Before you install

- **From 1.0 Beta, the app updates itself** (**Settings → Updates**). Your
  projects and models are kept.
- **Finding characters without a visible face costs time:** a new episode's
  analysis takes about a quarter longer, and episodes analysed before are
  looked through again for their people (about 3 minutes per hour of video
  on a mid-range GPU). It's off unless you turn it on; turning it off again
  leaves the cast exactly as with faces alone.
- **Two more models** (38 MB) download the first time the option is on.
  Like the face models, they come from InsightFace and may only be used
  non-commercially.
- **Not signed by a publisher yet.** On Windows, SmartScreen asks first:
  choose **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, right-click the app and choose
  **Open**, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AutoScene.app`.
